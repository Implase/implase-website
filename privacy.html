Privacy

This website is a static page. It does not use analytics, tracking cookies or contact forms, and it does not store anything about you.

Things that leave this site

  • Google Maps, Google Calendar and Google Forms embeds. These load from Google and are covered by Google's privacy policy. Google may set cookies when they load. The booking form sends the name, email, address, phone number and message you enter to the clinic's Google account, where only the clinic can read them.
  • Google Fonts. The Inter typeface is loaded from Google's servers, which see your IP address.
  • WhatsApp and phone links. Tapping them opens WhatsApp or your dialler. Anything you send goes to the clinic's number and is handled under WhatsApp's own terms.
  • GitHub Pages hosting. GitHub may log basic request data such as IP addresses. See GitHub's note on data collection.

Your records

Appointment details and dental records are kept at the clinic, not on this website. Ask at the clinic if you want to see or correct them.
