About Dr Sourav Chandra

BDS · MDS, Periodontology

Periodontist, laser dentistry specialist, oral implantologist and fellowship in aesthetic dentistry.

Dr Chandra has been leading Implase since 2018. He focuses on laser-assisted periodontics, which reduces bleeding and discomfort and speeds healing.

He is actively involved in academic and professional activities. He has contributed to published research concerning the use of lasers in the management of gum disease, particularly in patients with diabetes, hypertension, chronic kidney disorder and heart disease. In 2017, he was recognised with the Best Emerging Periodontist award at the India Dental Awards.

He aims to maintain transparency and prioritise patient care. Before treatment begins, he discusses the diagnosis, likely treatment options, and estimated costs with patients, so they can make informed decisions about their care.

Treatments

  • Laser dentistry

    Most gum and soft-tissue procedures done with a dental laser instead of a blade. Less bleeding, less pain and quicker healing.

  • Gum disease treatment

    Bleeding gums, bad breath and loose teeth are signs of gum disease. Deep cleaning, laser-assisted therapy and gum surgery when needed.

  • Scaling and polishing

    Professional cleaning that removes plaque and tartar above and below the gum line. Best done every six months.

  • Dental implants

    A fixed replacement for a missing tooth. Bone grafting where needed, careful placement and long-term care of the gum around the implant.

  • Braces and clear aligners

    Straightening for crowded, gapped or protruding teeth. Fixed metal braces, Damon self-ligating braces or clear aligners, chosen after a short assessment.

  • Veneers, laminates and zirconia crowns

    Thin porcelain facings that improve the shape and colour of front teeth, and strong metal-free zirconia crowns for damaged or root-treated teeth.

  • Mouth ulcers and lesions

    Laser removal of white patches, small growths and stubborn ulcers, and low-level laser therapy to ease pain and speed healing.

  • Children's and elderly dentistry

    Gentle first visits and preventive care for kids. For older patients: dentures, dry mouth, worn teeth and gum care at an unhurried pace.

  • Full-mouth rehabilitation

    One combined plan when several teeth and the gums all need work, using gum treatment, implants, crowns and laser care together.
